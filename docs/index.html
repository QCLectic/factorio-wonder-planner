About Factorio Planner

An unofficial fan tool for Factorio 2.x with Space Age. It is not made by or affiliated with Wube Software. Factorio is a trademark of Wube Software Ltd.

The data comes from the game's own data dump. This page contains no game art, icons, or sounds.

Limits

  • Gleba spoilage is not supported.
  • Not modeled: quality module output, drill quality, boiler steam, uranium mining acid, oil field yield other than 100%, and how items travel between planets.
  • Quality bonuses (+30% per level) come from the wiki, not the data dump. Beacon power is an upper bound.
  • Results are checked against hand-derived ratios, not yet against Factorio Lab or the wiki. Check important numbers yourself.

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. A shared link holds your whole plan in the part after the #, which servers do not receive.
